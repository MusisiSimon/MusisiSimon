Pay securely by card.

For clients settling a deposit, milestone or final invoice. Checkout runs on Stripe's own payment page — your card number is entered there, not here, and Intellora Tech never sees or stores it.

As stated on your invoice or proposal. Between $1.00 and $250,000.00.
🔒 TLS encrypted Processed by Stripe · PCI DSS Level 1 Visa · Mastercard · Amex accepted

How this works

  1. 1

    Enter the amount

    Use the figure from your invoice or written proposal. Add the reference so we can match the payment.

  2. 2

    You're redirected to Stripe

    Stripe's own checkout page opens. Card number, expiry and CVC are entered there, over an encrypted connection Stripe controls end to end.

  3. 3

    Instant confirmation

    You're returned here with an on-screen confirmation, and a receipt is emailed by Stripe automatically.

Why we don't take card numbers directly. Routing payment through Stripe's hosted checkout keeps Intellora Tech out of PCI-DSS scope entirely — the safest way to accept cards on a site this size, and the same standard used by companies far larger than us.
