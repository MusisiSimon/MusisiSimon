Working with us

How we handle your data and your risk.

Enterprise buyers ask these questions early, and the absence of an answer is disqualifying. Here is our current position, stated plainly — including where we are still building.

Access to your systems

Least-privilege access, requested per engagement and scoped to what the work needs. Named accounts only, never shared credentials. Access is revoked at handover and we confirm revocation in writing.

Confidentiality

Standard mutual NDAs signed usually within a day. Client data is never used to train models, never shared between engagements, and never retained after handover beyond what the contract requires.

Where work happens

Delivery is remote from Kampala, Uganda. Where your regulatory position requires processing in a specific jurisdiction, we design around it or tell you we are not the right supplier.

Subprocessors

We use no subprocessors for client data by default. If a programme requires partner capacity, the partner is named and approved by you in writing before any access is granted.

Conflicts of interest

The principal is currently employed by a national revenue authority. We disclose this in writing before any engagement in tax administration, customs or revenue, and step back from any procurement where a declarable conflict exists. Read the full position.

Insurance and formal certification

To confirm before launch. Professional indemnity cover and any ISO or SOC 2 alignment must be stated accurately here once arranged. We would rather leave this blank than claim cover we do not hold.

Capacity, honestly

What happens when the work is bigger than us.

Intellora Tech is a senior-led practice, not a body shop. That is the whole value proposition — you get depth and accountability rather than a rotating bench. It also means there is a real ceiling on how much can run in parallel.

Rather than quoting programmes we cannot staff, we do one of two things: phase the work into sequenced engagements each with its own fixed price, or deliver with a named partner team under a written consortium arrangement that you approve in advance. Our estimator applies the same rule — past a certain size it stops giving you a number and tells you to have a conversation instead.

Phased delivery

Sequenced engagements, each independently priced and independently valuable. You can stop after any phase.

Named partners

Additional capacity through a written arrangement, disclosed and approved before access is granted.

Consortium bids

For donor and public programmes, we bid as part of a consortium with clearly divided scope and named responsibility.

Honest refusal

Where neither route fits, we decline and say who might be better suited.

Proof

Client evidence.

This section is deliberately empty rather than filled with invented material. Everything here will be a real, attributable client outcome or it will not appear at all.

PLACEHOLDER — TESTIMONIAL

Two to three sentences from a client, attributed with full name, role and organisation. A LinkedIn recommendation from a former colleague is acceptable and honest if labelled as such.

PLACEHOLDER — CASE STUDY

Context, problem, constraints, approach, architecture, outcome, technologies and duration. Anonymised if the client requires it, but real throughout.

PLACEHOLDER — CLIENT LOGOS

Only logos of organisations that have given written permission. No placeholder marks, no "trusted by" claims without named clients behind them.

Why this is visible rather than hidden. An empty proof section is a smaller credibility problem than a fabricated one.
