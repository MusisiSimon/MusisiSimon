What happens on the call

  • Minutes 0–5 · What is actually brokenYou describe the problem. We ask who feels it and what it costs each month it continues.
  • Minutes 5–12 · Source system reality checkWhat data exists, how it is structured, and the state of the source systems. This is where estimates move most, so we do it early.
  • Minutes 12–17 · Budget and sequencingWhether your budget and our estimate are in the same range, and what order the work should happen in.
  • Minutes 17–20 · A direct answer on fitWhether we can help, what we would propose, and when you would get a written proposal.
Bring nothing prepared. An architecture diagram or a recent cloud bill speeds things up, but the call works fine without either.
We run three engagements at a time. If the slots are full when you write, we will tell you on the call and give you a realistic start date rather than taking the work and stretching it thin.

Before you write

What helps in a first email?

One paragraph on the problem, your rough stack, and any timing constraint. Nothing formal.

Will you sign an NDA before we talk?

Yes — standard mutual NDAs usually within a day.

Do you charge for the scoping call?

No. Deeper discovery producing a written scoping document is a paid engagement, and we say so before that line is crossed.

How do payments work internationally?

Invoices in USD, EUR, GBP or AED, paid by card or international wire. Typically fifty per cent on signing for a first engagement.

Tell us what is broken.

One paragraph is enough. You will hear back within one business day.
