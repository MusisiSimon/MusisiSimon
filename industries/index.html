Sectors, graded by how well we actually know them.

Most consultancies list forty industries and imply equal command of all of them. We do not, because a buyer who tests that claim finds the bottom quickly. The tiers below say plainly where we have production history, where the patterns transfer, and where we are competent but would not claim depth.

Why publish this at all? Because it is the same honesty you will get about your project. A supplier who is candid about the limits of their experience is a supplier who will tell you when an estimate is slipping.
