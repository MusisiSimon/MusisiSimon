06 · AWS Cloud Architecture

Architecture you can defend, and a bill you can predict.

Cloud cost problems are almost always architecture problems in disguise. A bill that grows faster than usage usually means full reloads where incremental would do, compute sized for a peak that no longer exists, or storage with no lifecycle policy. We treat the review and the savings as one engagement because they are one problem.

Where the savings usually are

In our experience of data platform estates, cost reduction opportunities cluster in a small number of places. An audit checks all of them.

  • Full table reloads where incremental processing would work
  • Compute provisioned for a peak load that no longer occurs
  • Development and test environments running outside working hours
  • Object storage with no lifecycle or tiering policy
  • Orphaned volumes, snapshots and load balancers nobody owns
  • Cross-availability-zone data transfer that architecture could avoid
  • On-demand pricing where committed use would apply cleanly
  • Query patterns that scan far more data than they return
On savings figures. We do not publish a headline percentage, because the honest answer depends entirely on your starting architecture. An estate already using incremental loads and committed pricing has far less headroom than one that is not. The audit quantifies your specific opportunity before anything is changed.

Common questions

Do you work with Azure or Google Cloud?
Our certified depth is on AWS and that is where our strongest work is. The architectural principles transfer, and we can review an Azure or GCP estate competently, but we would not claim equivalent depth and will tell you so before you engage us rather than after.
Can you work with our existing DevOps team?
Usually the best arrangement. We do the architecture and review work, your team executes with our support, and knowledge stays in-house. Some clients prefer we implement directly. Either works and we agree it at kickoff.
What if the review finds our architecture is broadly fine?
Then we say so, hand over a short report confirming it with the reasoning, and you have documented assurance for your board or your auditors. That has happened and it is a legitimate outcome — we are not incentivised to manufacture findings because the price is fixed before we start.
Can you migrate us with zero downtime?
For most data platforms we run the new environment in parallel with the old until reconciliation matches, then cut over. That is low-downtime rather than genuinely zero-downtime. Anyone promising zero downtime for a full data platform migration without seeing your architecture is guessing.
Do you resell AWS or take vendor commission?
No. We hold no reseller agreement and take no commission on your cloud spend. That matters specifically for cost work — our incentive is aligned with reducing your bill rather than growing it.
