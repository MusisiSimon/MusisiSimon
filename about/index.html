How a remote senior practice works

Weekly written update

Every Friday: what shipped, what is next, what is blocked, and any change to the estimate — in writing, so it survives being forwarded to your board.

Working sessions, not status calls

Calls are for decisions and joint work. Status arrives in writing beforehand so the call is not spent reading it aloud.

Your tools

We work in your Slack, your Jira, your repository and your cloud account, with access provisioned at least privilege and revoked on handover.

Handover as a deliverable

Documentation, runbooks and decision records, plus a live session with your engineers — so the work outlives the engagement.

Work with the engineer, not the org chart.

One paragraph about what is broken is enough to start.
