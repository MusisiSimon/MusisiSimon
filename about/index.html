Delivery

How a remote senior practice actually works.

Distributed delivery works when the communication discipline is explicit. Ours is written down and applied on every engagement regardless of size.

Weekly written update

Every Friday: what shipped, what is next, what is blocked, and any change to the estimate. In writing, so it survives being forwarded to your board.

Working sessions, not status calls

Calls are for decisions and joint work. Status arrives in writing beforehand so the call is not spent reading it aloud.

Your tools

We work in your repository, your ticket system and your chat. No proprietary portal you have to learn and then lose access to.

Escalation path

A named contact, an agreed response time, and a written escalation route agreed at kickoff rather than improvised during a problem.

Decision records

Architecture decisions are written down with the alternatives considered, so in six months your team knows why the system is shaped this way.

Midpoint demo

Every engagement over three weeks has a working demonstration at the halfway point. Surprises should arrive early and cheaply.

Handover as a deliverable

Runbooks, documentation and a live session with your engineers. Handover is scoped and priced, never an afterthought at the end.

Thirty days after

Follow-up questions answered at no cost for thirty days after handover. Long enough for your team to hit the real questions.

Disclosure

Conflicts of interest.

This is stated in full rather than buried, because in our strongest sector it is the first thing a serious buyer should ask about.

The position

The principal is currently employed by a national revenue authority. Our deepest domain experience — tax administration, customs and revenue analytics — comes directly from that work, and that same fact creates an obvious question about independence when the client is a revenue or customs body.

What we do about it

  • We disclose the employment in writing before any engagement touching tax administration, customs or revenue administration — proactively, not on request.
  • We complete any conflict-of-interest declaration a tender requires in full rather than narrowly.
  • We step back from any specific procurement where a declarable conflict exists, including where the conflict is one of appearance rather than substance.
  • We maintain strict separation and confidentiality between concurrent engagements.
  • No employer system, dataset, network or working time is ever used for client work.
  • Client work is delivered entirely outside employer hours on separate personal infrastructure.
An honest limitation. There are engagements we cannot take while this employment continues, and no disclosure process changes that. Where a procurement is one of them, we will say so early rather than bid and withdraw. If independence from any revenue authority is a hard requirement for you, we are not the right supplier and we would rather tell you in the first ten minutes.
